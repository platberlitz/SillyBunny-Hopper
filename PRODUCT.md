# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SillyBunny roleplayers managing a personal cast of characters, primarily during focused desktop sessions, with the same essential controls available on mobile.

## Product Purpose

Twitlike creates a private, fictional social timeline for the user's persona and invited characters. Success means the cast is quick to curate, generated activity stays recognizably in character, and timeline events can enrich chats without pretending anything was posted publicly.

## Brand Personality

Playful, candid, trustworthy. The interface should feel native to SillyBunny and disappear into the social-roleplay task.

## Anti-references

Avoid a hard-coded Twitter-blue identity, dark-only styling, glassmorphism, decorative card grids, noisy motion, hidden model activity, and unfamiliar controls where native form elements work.

## Design Principles

- Inherit the host theme instead of imposing a separate visual system.
- Make choosing the active cast fast even with a large character library.
- Keep generated activity clearly fictional and under the user's control.
- Make persistence, model work, failures, and recovery explicit.
- Keep common posting and reading workflows compact on desktop and complete on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and semantics. Support complete keyboard operation, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion preferences, theme contrast, and 44px touch targets for coarse pointers.
